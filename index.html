01 / ABOUT

From stagecraft to screen.

Aaron Pierre is an English actor trained at the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art. His screen work includes The Underground Railroad, Old, Foe, Rebel Ridge, and Mufasa: The Lion King. His career also includes stage work, including his professional debut as Cassio in Othello.
